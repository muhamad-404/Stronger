# Stronger — User Guide

A plain-language guide to using **Stronger** properly. This document explains every part of the app: what it is for, how each screen works, and how to build a calm daily rhythm.

> **User guide vs Guide**  
> This **User guide** teaches you how to use Stronger’s screens and flows.  
> The in-app **Guide** (More → Guide) is a library of articles about food, training, sleep, hard days, and safety. Use both when you need them.

Stronger is also available inside the app: **More → How to use Stronger**.

---

## Welcome to Stronger

Stronger is a gentle companion for healthy weight gain. It helps you:

- Plan meals with familiar home foods
- Do simple strength training at home
- Sleep a little more steadily
- Notice progress over weeks

It does **not** use calorie counting, punishing diets, or guilt when a day is imperfect.

The app is designed to feel calm and practical. You use it on your phone or computer in the browser. You can also install it like an app on your home screen.

### Not medical advice

Stronger shares practical habits and ideas. It does **not** diagnose illness, prescribe medicine, or replace a doctor, dietitian, or other healthcare professional. If you have concerning symptoms, unexplained weight loss, pain, dizziness, or anything that worries you, please talk with a professional.

### What to expect

- No calorie targets or food “scores”
- Focus on consistency: regular meals, gentle strength, sleep, and patience
- Pakistani and home-style food ideas, plus everyday options like eggs, yogurt, oats, and shakes
- Hard days are expected — Problem solver and Guide are there when things feel stuck

---

## Things to know before you start

Everything you enter — weight, food, workouts, sleep, notes — **stays on this device** in your browser. Stronger does **not** upload your personal health or progress data to a server.

### Protect your progress

Because data lives on this device, clearing site data, uninstalling the browser, switching phones without a backup, or running out of storage can erase your history.

**Export a backup regularly** from **More → Settings → Data & backup**.

### Good habits

- Use the same browser (and same phone/computer) when you can
- Export a backup file every week or two and keep it somewhere safe
- If you get a new phone, import that backup file before you start fresh
- Stronger works offline for daily use once the page has loaded; install it for easier access

## First-time setup

The first time you open Stronger, you go through a short setup. Answer honestly — you can change most of this later in Settings.

1. **Your name** — A preferred name so Today can greet you. It stays on this device.
2. **Current weight** — Your starting weight in kilograms. This begins your weight history.
3. **Height** — Optional context for your profile. You can update it later.
4. **Goal weight** — Where you hope to move toward over time. Stronger also suggests a gentler first milestone so the path feels reachable.
5. **Wake and bedtime** — Rough times you usually wake and sleep. These shape your daily routine checklist and sleep defaults.
6. **Confirm and begin** — Review your answers, finish setup, and land on **Today** — your home screen.

If you leave mid-setup, Stronger tries to remember where you were so you can continue later on the same device.

## Today — your daily home

Today is where most days should begin. It shows a greeting, how your weight is trending toward your first milestone, and a simple checklist for the day.

### What you will see

1. **Weight progress card** — A calm look at where you are versus your starting point and first milestone. Detailed logging lives under Progress.
2. **Today checklist** — Tap each row (meals, snacks, workout or rest, sleep cues, and similar items based on your routine) to mark it done. Partial days still count — consistency beats perfection.
3. **Daily progress** — Shows how many checklist items you finished today.
4. **Quick actions** — Jump straight to logging food, logging weight, starting a workout, opening Need help (Problem solver), or adding a short note for today.
5. **Tip of the day** — A small practical nudge — open it when you want inspiration, skip it when you do not.
6. **Weekly consistency** — A simple view of recent days so you can see rhythm without obsessing over one off day.

**In the app:** open the **Today** tab.

## Eat — food without calorie counting

Eat is your food home. Stronger never asks you to count calories. Instead it helps you notice meals and snacks, pick familiar foods, and keep going on low-appetite days.

### How the food flow works

1. **Food home** — See today’s meal and snack consistency at a glance, open the full food log, or jump into breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, shakes, and more.
2. **Appetite is low?** — The banner at the top of Eat opens gentle ideas and softer foods when eating feels hard.
3. **Food log (Today’s log)** — Open “Open log” or “Food log.” Switch dates if you need to fix yesterday. Add foods with Add, mark items eaten, edit or remove entries, and use meal ideas to add a suggested meal quickly.
4. **Browse meal ideas** — Browse by category (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and so on). Open a meal to read ingredients and notes, then add it to your log when it fits.
5. **Boosters** — Simple ways to make meals more nourishing (for example adding milk, nuts, oil, or yogurt) without forcing huge portions.
6. **Food problems** — Shortcuts into common food struggles; deeper help also lives in Problem solver and the Guide articles.

### A good food day

Aim for regular meals and snacks you can actually finish or mostly finish. If you only manage a shake and a few bites, log that honestly — it still helps you see patterns.

**In the app:** open the **Eat** tab.

## Move — gentle strength at home

Move is built around about **three gentle strength sessions a week** — not hard cardio. Two programs alternate: **Workout A** and **Workout B**. Rest days are part of the plan.

### How training works

1. **Train kindly reminder** — Read the safety points on the Move home. Stop if something feels sharp or wrong, and seek professional advice when needed.
2. **Today’s plan** — Shows whether today is a workout day or a rest day. On workout days you can open the workout or tap **Start workout**.
3. **Workout A and Workout B** — Open either program to see the full list of exercises. Tap an exercise for how-to guidance.
4. **Running a session** — Start the session, check off sets as you go, note what you actually did if it differs from the target, mark exercises complete, then **finish** the workout so it saves to your history.
5. **Rest days** — No strength session is required. A gentle walk is optional. Recovery supports progress as much as training.

### Missed a workout?

Do not punish yourself with a double session. Open **Problem solver → “I missed my workout”** or simply do the next scheduled day when you can.

**In the app:** open the **Move** tab.

## Progress — trends, not daily pressure

Progress shows the bigger picture. Weight moves slowly and can bounce day to day — Stronger emphasizes trends and consistency, not one weigh-in.

### What you can do here

1. **Log weight** — Use Log weight on the summary card. Weigh at a similar time of day when you can (for example morning). One skipped day is fine.
2. **Weight chart** — A simple chart of recent weigh-ins so you can see direction over time.
3. **Goals snapshot** — Edit your main weight-related goals here, or open the full Goals page for food, strength, sleep, and routine goals.
4. **Consistency** — How steadily you have been logging food, movement, and related habits — a rhythm check, not a grade.
5. **Journal check-in** — Quickly rate energy and appetite, note sleep hours/quality, and leave a short note for today.
6. **Weekly review** — Open the full weekly review to look back calmly and write a short reflection.

**In the app:** open the **Progress** tab.

## Problem solver — help for hard moments

When appetite is quiet, a meal is missed, energy is low, or the week went sideways, open **Problem solver**. Choose the situation that fits best. You will see:

- Things to try today
- What to try next
- What not to worry about
- When it may be wise to seek professional advice

### Situations covered

- I don’t feel hungry
- I feel full quickly
- I couldn’t finish my meal
- I missed a meal
- I feel too tired to exercise
- I missed my workout
- I slept badly
- I don’t have time to prepare food
- I don’t know what to eat
- I am struggling to eat enough
- My routine was disrupted

### How to use it well

Pick **one** small action from “try this today.” Do not try to fix everything at once. Related Guide articles appear at the bottom when you want to read more.

**In the app:** More → **Problem solver** (or Today → **Need help**).

## Sleep & recovery

Sleep supports appetite, energy, and training. Open **Sleep & recovery** from More.

- Log tonight or last night with bedtime, wake time, and how the night felt
- Your week view shows rhythm without demanding perfect nights
- Use your usual wake and bed times from setup as a starting point
- A rough log is better than none — estimate if you must
- Read the practical habits on the page, or open the full sleep article in Guide
- If you slept badly, Problem solver has a dedicated path

**In the app:** More → **Sleep & recovery**.

## Weekly review

Once a week (for example Sunday evening or Monday morning), open **Weekly review** from More or Progress. Stronger summarizes weight change, food consistency, workouts, and sleep for that week.

1. **Browse weeks** — Move to previous weeks if you want history. The current week updates as you keep logging.
2. **Read the metrics** — Treat them as information, not a score. Flat weight weeks happen. Missed workouts happen.
3. **Write a reflection** — What went well, what was difficult, what you might change next, optional notes, and a focus for next week.
4. **Save** — So your reflection stays with that week on this device.

**In the app:** More → **Weekly review** (or from Progress).

## Goals — more than the scale

Goals live under More (and are linked from Progress). Weight goals matter, but Stronger also encourages goals like regular breakfasts, finishing workouts, steadier sleep, or a calmer routine.

1. **Add a goal** — Tap add, choose a type, write a clear title, and set a simple target you can measure.
2. **Update progress** — Open a goal and update how far along you are as the week goes.
3. **Celebrate completion** — When a goal completes, acknowledge it — then set a new gentle goal if you like.
4. **Completed list** — You can show past completed goals so wins are not forgotten.

**In the app:** More → **Goals**.

## Settings — profile, routine, data, and app

Open **More → Settings**. Your details stay on this device.

1. **Profile** — Update name, height, weight-related details, and goal weight when life changes.
2. **Goals (settings)** — Adjust milestone-style weight goals connected to your profile.
3. **Routine** — Change wake time and bedtime so Today and Sleep match your real life.
4. **Data & backup** — Export a backup file, import a previous backup, see storage notes, or clear all local data (this resets the app — export first).
5. **App** — Install Stronger to your home screen when your browser offers it, and read about privacy and the app.

### Clearing data

**Clear all data** erases progress on this device and returns you to setup. Only do this if you mean to start over — and only after exporting a backup if you might want the old data later.

**In the app:** More → **Settings**.

## A simple daily rhythm

How a typical day can look when using Stronger well:

1. Open **Today**. Check your greeting and checklist.
2. Eat breakfast (or a shake / soft option). Log it in **Eat**.
3. Through the day: log lunch, dinner, and snacks when you can. Use Browse if you need ideas.
4. On a workout day: open **Move → Start workout**. On a rest day: rest without guilt.
5. Log weight a few times a week from **Progress** (not necessarily every day).
6. In the evening: mark sleep-related checklist items, log sleep when you wake, or journal briefly on Progress.
7. If something feels hard: **More → Problem solver** (or Need help on Today).

**Once a week:** open Weekly review, read the summary, write a short reflection, and pick one gentle focus for next week.

**Every week or two:** More → Settings → Data & backup → export a backup.

## Tips for using Stronger well

- Log imperfect days. Incomplete meals and missed workouts are useful information, not failure.
- Prefer steady meals over “perfect” plates.
- Use low-appetite tools early instead of waiting until you feel worse.
- Strength + rest beats random hard cardio for this style of plan.
- Judge progress over weeks, not single mornings on the scale.
- Read Guide articles when curious; use Problem solver when stuck in the moment.
- Keep backups. Your future self will thank you.
- Install the app on your phone if you can, so Stronger is one tap away.

## Common questions

### Do I have to finish every checklist item?

No. The checklist is a guide. Doing most of it most days is the aim.

### Why doesn’t Stronger show calories?

On purpose. The focus is regular nourishing food and habits you can keep, not number chasing.

### Can I use Stronger on two phones?

Data does not sync automatically. Export a backup from one device and import it on the other if you need to move.

### What if my weight goes down or stays flat?

Short-term dips and plateaus are common. Check food consistency, sleep, stress, and illness. Keep logging and review the week. Seek professional help if weight loss is unexplained or you feel unwell.

### Is the Guide the same as this User guide?

No. This User guide teaches the app. The Guide teaches food, training, sleep, hard days, and safety topics.

### What if I feel unwell or unsafe?

Stop and seek appropriate medical help. Open **Guide → Safety** for when to seek help. Stronger is not emergency care.
